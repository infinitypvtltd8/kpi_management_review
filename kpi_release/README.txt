KPI - MANAGEMENT REVIEW | DASHBOARD
====================================

PACKAGE CONTENTS
- index.html: the complete single-file dashboard, including the two logos.
- README.txt: these instructions.

PUBLISH ON GITHUB PAGES
1. Extract the ZIP and upload or replace index.html in the ROOT of your GitHub repository (not inside a folder). The name must be exactly index.html.
2. Commit the change to the main branch.
3. In the repository, open Settings > Pages. Under Build and deployment choose Deploy from a branch, then select main and /(root), and save.
4. Open the resulting HTTPS Pages URL after deployment. This ZIP does not update your repository by itself.

USE LIVE DATA AND REPORTS
- Choose APEIRO or X GUARD. The app loads data from the published employee tabs and checks again every five minutes while visible. You can also press Refresh.
- Open Reports. To include all data currently available for both companies, select "Both companies - whole group" and "All available months", then choose "Download all reports". The ZIP is generated on your device and includes an Excel workbook, 14 CSV reports, a manifest and a methodology note.
- To download one report, select its card and press Download CSV. Print full report / PDF uses your browser's print dialog; choose Save as PDF there. Other dashboard views can also be printed.
- Reports are snapshots, not live files. Refresh and export again to capture later changes.
- An app/file preview or an HTML file opened directly from your computer may block the Google Sheet connection. Use an internet-connected HTTPS hosting page or the live server preview.

DATA AND PRIVACY
- The Google Sheet is publicly published, and a public GitHub Pages site is public. The HTML contains the staff roster and published Sheet URL. Employee, operational and financial data may therefore be accessible to others. For confidential use, use authenticated hosting and a private data source; this static app is not access control.
- Downloaded reports contain individual KPI details and potentially sensitive notes. Keep reports private. Do not upload the reports ZIP, CSVs or workbook to a public repository.
- Missing and unavailable information is not zero. Stale tabs are labelled. Duplicate month sections are visible but excluded from totals; conflicting scores or statuses are flagged and excluded from affected averages or assessments.
- Month headings without years stay unspecified. Unit review groups and work themes are based on the supplied roles and keyword inference; confirm official definitions. Averages combine different KPI types and are indicative rather than like-for-like rankings. Projections are illustrative, not predictions.
